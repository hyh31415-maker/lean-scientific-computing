# Scientific Probe Output Contract

Use only the sections that materially help the task. Keep the report proportional to the experiment.

## Research contract

- Scientific question
- H0, H1, and relevant alternatives
- Discriminating observable
- Minimal experiment
- Predeclared decision rule
- Stop condition
- Invalidating assumptions

## Execution record

- Data or model provenance
- Exact command
- Environment or dependency versions that affect the result
- Seed or seed set
- Raw output location
- Deviations from the research contract, labeled as exploratory

## Result

- `[observed]` What happened
- `[derived]` What follows under stated assumptions
- `[hypothesis]` What explanation gained or lost support
- `[speculation]` What remains merely possible
- Decision: supports, weakens, falsifies, or inconclusive
- Sensitivity and known failure modes

## Complexity ledger

For each changed or added file, give one sentence describing its epistemic or reproducibility role.

List notable engineering deliberately omitted, such as packaging, generic configuration, APIs, dashboards, broad refactors, and future-proof abstractions.

## Stop declaration

State why the current stop condition has or has not been reached. Do not continue polishing after it is reached.
