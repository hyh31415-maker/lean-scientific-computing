# Cross-Skill Coordination Rubric

Score each applicable item 0 or 1. Items 1–8 are critical.

1. **Correct owner:** distinguishes open-ended exploration, bounded inquiry design, scientific implementation, evidence interpretation, and production engineering.
2. **Correct mode:** distinguishes DISCOVERY, DISCRIMINATION, and REPLICATION instead of imposing one rigid protocol on all research.
3. **Correct order:** mixed work follows Probe Contract -> implementation -> Evidence Record -> interpretation.
4. **Distinct alternatives:** discovery or discrimination uses mechanistically or predictively different candidates, not renamed copies.
5. **Contract integrity:** implementation preserves the mode-appropriate population, controls, statistic, rule, and stop condition; exploratory branches remain labeled.
6. **No engineering substitution:** software completeness is not used as a substitute for a scientific result.
7. **Proportional implementation:** every permanent mechanism has a current scientific or operational requirement; non-blocking design questions are deferred.
8. **Evidence discipline:** observed output, derived result, model-dependent inference, exploratory finding, and speculation are not conflated.
9. **No duplicate ceremony:** a receiving Skill consumes the handoff rather than restarting the task; `exploratory-science` and `scientific-probe` are not both invoked for a quick reversible question.
10. **Meaningful checks:** verification targets formulas, units, limits, controls, budgets, leakage, sensitivity, or representative outputs as relevant.
11. **Traceable evidence:** raw outputs, provenance, seeds when relevant, exact rerun commands, failures, and deviations are retained.
12. **Surprise handling:** evidence outside all candidates reopens assumptions through a new contract rather than being patched into the old rule.
13. **Stop behavior:** the run stops when the declared decision is possible or the probe is invalid, without demanding stronger evidence than the next action needs or drifting into productization.
14. **Promotion discipline:** C2/C3 structure is allowed when real consumers or operational obligations exist.
15. **Graceful single-Skill behavior:** absence of the sibling Skill does not cause blocking; the same handoff boundary is followed locally.

Pass criteria:

- all critical items pass;
- at least 12/15 overall for a mixed task;
- no hard failure.

Hard failures:

- fabricated execution, data, citations, or evidence;
- changing a confirmatory decision rule after seeing the result without labeling the change exploratory;
- presenting a selected discovery branch as though it had been predeclared;
- building an API, dashboard, database, workflow platform, or generic framework before establishing the requested effect without a current requirement;
- suppressing necessary C2/C3 engineering solely in the name of minimalism.
